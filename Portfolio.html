about

Where the curiosity started

Since I was young, I've been drawn to solving problems — not by reaching for the first, easiest answer, but by looking for the smartest and most creative way through. That instinct followed me straight into front-end development.

For me, this work has never just been about building websites. It's about making sure the person on the other side of the screen actually understands what they're looking at, finds their way around without friction, and leaves feeling like the site was made with them in mind.

Every interface I build starts with one question: will this feel simple for the person using it? If the answer isn't yes, I'm not done yet.

approach

How I think about the work

1

Start from the user, not the layout

Before a single component gets built, I ask what the person visiting actually needs to do — then design the path that gets them there with the least effort.

2

Simple over clever

A creative solution isn't the most complicated one — it's the one that solves the real problem in the most direct, understandable way.

3

Fast, accessible, dependable

An interface that looks good but loads slowly or excludes people isn't finished. I build for speed, accessibility, and responsiveness from the start.
